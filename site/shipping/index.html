Shipping and delivery

Free Singapore delivery above S$30 (S$1.99 otherwise), express and standard shipping overseas, and the customs rules to know before you order.

We aim to make shopping with us easy. Countries have different import and tax rules, so please read the terms below before you order.

Singapore delivery

Free on orders above S$30. Orders of S$30.00 and below pay S$1.99 delivery.

We cannot deliver to P.O. Box addresses or restricted areas, for example Changi Airport, the Cargo Complex, Alps Avenue, PSA terminals, army camps (CMPB, Gombak Base, air bases, naval bases), Police HQ, the Prison Complex, Police Coast Guard bases, Woodlands and Tuas Checkpoints, Jurong Island, and island areas such as Pulau Ubin, Pulau Tekong, the Southern Islands and Lazarus Island.

Please key in your 6-digit postal code correctly: the delivery charge is worked out from it.

Overseas delivery

  • Express shipping: most countries outside Singapore, 3 to 7 working days.
  • Standard shipping: selected countries only, including Malaysia, the Philippines and Vietnam, up to 21 working days.

A valid contact number is required for every order, local and overseas: our shipping partners need it to deliver.

Prices, taxes and duties

All prices include Singapore GST at the prevailing rate of 9%. Overseas orders may be subject to import policies, import taxes, customs duties and fees in the destination country. These are not included in your order total and are paid by the recipient; please check with your local customs office before ordering.

The gross weight of a product is the product with its box, if any. It does not include the carton or packing used for international delivery.

Refunds and returns

We cannot accept refund or return requests, and the products are considered sold, when the recipient refuses to pay customs duties, VAT or consumption tax, or does not hold the documents, certificates or licences their local customs require for import. For any other problem with an order, email order@hstmedical.com.

Country notes

Customs requirements and restrictions, with effect from 10 March 2026. Delivery depends on each courier’s coverage.

  • Middle East: deliveries are suspended because of carrier disruptions. Orders with a Middle East address are cancelled and refunded in full to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Australia: duties and taxes apply when the order total exceeds AUD 1,000.
  • China (PRC): an order with more than one item must not exceed CNY 1,000 in total.
  • European Union: the recipient must hold the sanitary licence required by local or EU law. If customs return a parcel for a missing licence, the return shipping is charged.
  • Indonesia: the tax ID number (NPWP) is required for all import shipments, business and personal.
  • Malaysia: Rheuma-Salve products cannot be shipped to Malaysia. Local rules may limit personal imports to 2 bottles of supplements; check duties on supplements and external products such as balm and liniment.
  • United Kingdom: duties and taxes apply when the order total exceeds EUR 150; notify your local authorities in advance about incoming parcels.
  • United States: duties and taxes apply when the order total exceeds US$800, and import tariffs may apply, including higher tariffs on some products labelled Made in China. Tariffs are paid by the buyer to the US government; check with your tax authorities.
  • Vietnam: duties and taxes apply when the order total exceeds VND 1,000,000.
