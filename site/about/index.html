About HST Medical

Singapore’s health supplements and pain relief company since 1994, with roots in a family medical hall founded in 1930, and part of Kowa Pharmaceutical Asia since 2026.

HST Medical (博诚药业) is a Singapore health supplements and pain relief company, best known for Rheuma-Salve® Pain Relief Balm, one of Singapore’s iconic balms. Over the years the name has come to stand for Higher, Stronger, Together: the aspiration to go higher, be stronger and get together to do great things for the world, and it all starts with one’s holistic health.

A heritage that began in 1930

The name is a tribute to Heng Say Tong, the medical hall established by the family of the company’s co-founders. HST Medical Pte Ltd was incorporated in 1994 and draws on nearly a century of heritage in healthcare.

  • 1930: Heng Say Tong medical hall, the family business behind the name
  • 1994: HST Medical Pte Ltd incorporated in Singapore
  • 2026: joined Kowa Pharmaceutical Asia, part of the Kowa group

The best of East and West

HST Medical brings together professionals from Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and Western pharmacological backgrounds, who formulate supplements that are easy to take and affordable for everyone. The range covers pain relief, kids’ vitamins, immunity support for colds and flu, joint support, beauty and much more.

Quality and safety first

From research and development to packaging and manufacturing, HST Medical follows authority guidelines, approved procedures and inspected premises. Ingredients are tested for authenticity, finished products are verified for safety, and third-party manufacturing is audited.

Part of Kowa Pharmaceutical Asia

On 29 May 2026 HST Medical became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Kowa Pharmaceutical Asia Pte. Ltd., part of Japan’s Kowa group. The HST Medical name and the existing leadership team stay: Ellis Tan continues as General Manager, and co-founder Simone Tan serves as Director and Head of Business Development, supporting President Nikhil Kapur. With Kowa, HST Medical is preparing to take Rheuma-Salve®, Heritage®, HST Medical® and Zoo-Vite® across ASEAN and beyond.

Home of Rheuma-Salve® and Zoo-Vite®

For backache, muscle sprains or strains, arthritis, rheumatism and other joint pains there is the Rheuma-Salve® range: balm, liniment, patch, crème and Medi-Stick, with travel packs too. For children, the Zoo-Vite® vitamins range was featured by Sassy Mama in “Picky Eaters? Why Multivitamins Can Help Fill Your Kid’s Nutrient Gap”.
