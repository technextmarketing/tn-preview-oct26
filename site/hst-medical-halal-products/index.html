HST Medical’s Halal products

Published April 11, 2025 by HST Medical (local)

Did you know that health supplements can be halal-certified, in addition to food and drink? What does halal mean? And why are halal health supplements popular? Let’s find out.

Halal means…

Halal in Arabic means “permissible” or “lawful.” Halal is defined by Islamic law as what Muslims can consume or interact with. Halal addresses health supplements, among other things, beyond food and drink. The opposite of halal is haram, which in Arabic means “forbidden” or “unlawful.”

Halal certification ensures that items meet Islamic requirements and is a demanding process. Something is halal if:

  • Steer clear of non-halal animal items, including pork and hog byproducts.
  • Halal-approved production techniques, with halal-certified instruments, storage, and transportation, among other things.
  • Prevent product contamination, particularly with regard to cross-contamination with non-halal compounds.

Benefits

Halal health supplements provide outstanding goods fit for consumers’ values and way of life, regardless of culture, race, or religion. The products have to conform to demanding processes, as well as serve diet- or ingredient-conscious consumers. Halal health supplements are premium products that comply with halal certifications with ingredients and manufacturing processes that all comply to earn such certifications.
